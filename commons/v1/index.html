A suggested way forward for property packs in England. One worked proposal — not a standard, and endorsed by nobody.

Nothing is published at this address yet. It is reserved, licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0, and covered by the editorial gate from the commit that created it — so that no row can ever land here ungated and need grandfathering in afterwards.

What is expected to land here:

  • question-bank/ — modules of questions, authored from scratch, each carrying who asks for it and why
  • legislation-watch.json — what is being watched, on what cadence, and which components a status change would move
  • gap-catalogue.json — for every component: who holds it, and what the next step is
  • readiness-model.json — the completeness formula, credit bands and applicability rules, with the initial weights published as the guesses they are
  • language-rulebook.json — never-say and say-instead pairs, as data rather than prose
  • leasehold-request.md — an information request template a managing agent would recognise

Every row published here will carry a primary sourceUrl, a status from the five-value taxonomy, checkedBy, lastCheckedAt and jurisdiction. Continuous integration enforces all five on every commit.
